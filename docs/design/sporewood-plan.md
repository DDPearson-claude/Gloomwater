# Sporewood Pools: the plan

Approved by the designer on 24 November 2026 (decisions.md, "Sporewood: the direction" and "Sporewood: the hunt"). The rule
for the whole lake: **the same loop as Mossmere, about as much content, its own story and its own fish.** Where this plan
does not say, do what Mossmere does, in Sporewood's own words and look. Facts about the lake today are in the two studies
listed at the foot.

## The story: the island that wanders

Burr was the last seed-keeper of the old gardens. He planted his best seeds on a mossy islet in the pools. By morning the
islet had gone. It has been seen since, never in the same place twice. It is not an islet. It is the **Meadowback Pacu**, a
pacu so old that moss and little caps grow on her back, and around the lake she is called **the wandering island**. A pacu
eats seeds and nuts and cracks them with teeth like a person's. That is the heart of the case.

The hunt asks Mossmere's three questions:

- **Where she is.** She has no fixed home. Each dusk the Mother Cap sighs and lets fall its seed pods (the **seedfall**),
  and the spore drift carries them to one of four eddies. She feeds where they gather. The player reads the drift.
- **Which fish she is not.** A line-up of five crushers, crossed out as they are caught and their marks compared with the
  cracked pods: a beak, crushing plates, razor teeth, pads. Hers are teeth like a person's.
- **What gear holds her.** Lessons from lost fights. She crushes an ordinary hook, bites through a plain knot, and wears out
  a weak line.

The ending: Murk's line first, then the keepsake, **Burr's Seed** (it grows into a little meadow on the raft; in later
lakes a giant bites 1.5 times as often: a filled gap, the designer may change it). A bounty of **3,000** clams into
Sporewood's purse (Sporewood earns about four times Mossmere, whose bounty is 900). Landing her opens the third water,
Glasswater, when it exists; until then the ending says so plainly. Her rematch grows harder as Mossmere's does.

Pacing, as Mossmere's: gifts on evenings 1 and 2, the tale on evening 2, Burr on evening 3, the island rises on evening 4
or 5, the refusal on 5 or 6, losses from 6 to 9, the landing from 7 to 12 for a keen player.

## Places: four to five

The Lullaby and the Gill Harp (free), the Veilwater and the Mother's Cup (maps), and **the Drowned Greenhouse**, Burr's
home: rows of glass under the water, found by sailing to Burr's lamp (a title and the mist lifting, as Mossmere's finds).
Its own fish is the Razorpane Piranha. The Mother's Cup is where the seedfall comes from.

## Fish: fourteen to twenty-four

- **Lake list, 9 to 12:** the three left out come back: Sporebelly Puffer, Plumgill Perch, Hushcap Koi.
- **Place fish, 2 to 3:** the Sleepspore Jelly (Lullaby), the Nightbloom Carp (Veilwater), and the **Razorpane Piranha**
  (the Greenhouse; razor teeth; a suspect).
- **Giants, 2:** the Harpstring Eel and the Festival Puffer (its beak makes it a suspect).
- **Local kinds, 0 to 4**, each a quarter of one base fish's bites in one home, as Mossmere's: Lantern Fry (Netcloud Fry,
  the Veilwater), Gillstring Barb (Falsesmile Barb, the Gill Harp), Dewshell Crayfish (Capshell Crayfish, the Lullaby),
  Cupwell Catfish (Downcast Catfish, the Mother's Cup).
- **Visitors, 0 to 2:** the **Seedfall Silver Dollar**, a small seed-eater with teeth like a person's that comes only with
  the seedfall (its card is the clue that points to her kind); and one Mossmere fish brought by a notebook find, as the
  Nightbloom Carp goes the other way.
- **The legend:** the Meadowback Pacu.
- **The five suspects:** Festival Puffer (beak), Sporebelly Puffer (beak), Velvet Ray (crushing plates), Razorpane
  Piranha (razor teeth), Cindertail Catfish (pads).

## Tackle: Sporewood's own rig parts

Sporewood keeps its rods, lines, reels, lures, creels and raft parts. Its rig parts grow from 3 to 8, like Mossmere's:
Cork Float and **Drift Float**; Plain Knot, **Brass Trace** (toothy fish) and **Mossthread Snood** (wary fish); Mere Hook
and **Burl Hook** (it does not crush); the **Seed Pouch** with Capseeds. Sporewood does not use depth: the drift is its own
skill, as the bottom is Mossmere's. Practice orders for its four new parts, as Mossmere's. Prices follow Sporewood's
earnings: the first rod, line and reel steps are 350 on the shelf (done); the hunt's parts about four times Mossmere's
(Burl Hook about 150, Brass Trace about 230); the hunt's line is the Mothsilk.

## Signs, words and look

- **Signs on the water** (Mossmere's `tells` and `sight`; Sporewood has none): rises, swirls and sightings of its own.
- **The drift is drawn** on the water at every quality setting, since play depends on it.
- **Words:** Murk's lines for the lake from 7 to about 23, Burr's about 12, the opening card for a new lake, the hunt's
  lines, all in the voice guide's style.
- **Look:** Mossmere's look pass for Sporewood: light by the hour (day, dusk and night must read differently), the water,
  its own plants (lily rafts at the Lullaby, the hanging gill-strings at the Gill Harp, mushrooms), mist, props.

## Order of work

1. Groundwork: the hunt's code made per lake, with no change seen in Mossmere; the three left-out fish; the price cut.
2. The look pass (it touches only the 3D scene).
3. The drift, the seedfall, the Drift Float, the Seed Pouch and Capseeds, the new rig parts, practice orders, signs.
4. The new fish and the Drowned Greenhouse.
5. The hunt's data and words, Burr, the scenes, the notepad's sketches, the ending.
6. A words pass, a timed play-through, a checklist like `mossmere-100.md`, and the full test suite.
