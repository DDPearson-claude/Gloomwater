# Mossmere at 100%: a checklist for the designer to edit

This is a proposal: add, strike or reword anything. Each item has a plain test for "done". "Testers" means three to five people who are told nothing beforehand, playing a timed session.

## The hunt

| ☐ | Item | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| ☑ | Her new name is used everywhere | Journal, hook banner, Murk, hints and the landing card all use the name you chose, and the old epithet appears nowhere |
| ☐ | She can be found without help | At least half the testers land her, and can say why they fished the Bell Pool after the midnight bell, on the bottom, with a minnow |
| ☐ | The hunt is the right length | Testers' median landing falls between evening 7 and 11; nobody quits a heading after three nudges |
| ☐ | Every evening adds something | From evening 2 until the landing, no tester has an evening without a new case line |
| ☑ | Nothing gives the answer away | Her locked card, the Odds page, the shelf and the map show nothing before its clue arrives |
| ☐ | The bell works at night | On a phone, testers notice the midnight bell, and know it is time to fish for her |
| ☑ | Landing pays off | Measuring, release, bounty, keepsake, gilt stone and Sporewood chart all happen on a fresh save and on an old one |
| ☑ | Old saves are safe | A save that bought the Bell Pool, one holding Heartmoss and one that already sold her all load and make sense |
| ☑ | Sporewood is untouched | Only the way in has changed; a Sporewood evening plays exactly as before |

## The gear

| ☐ | Item | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| ☑ | The default rig is unchanged | Seeded 100-cast comparisons on the cork match today's build in every area |
| ☐ | A bottom bite reads on the handheld | You can see and hear a nibble and a bite on the lead from the raft, with the sound on and with it off |
| ☐ | The rig picture is clear | After one look, testers can say what each part does and what limits their rig |
| ☑ | Failures name the part | Every refusal and escape names one part, and the picture marks it |
| ☐ | Live bait is easy | Testers put a minnow in the tin and fish it without being told how |

## Controls, rewards and approvals

|---|---|---|

## Faults the code maps found

| ☐ | Fault | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| ☑ | A load error may skip some lake scenery, possibly including the giant's back in the mist | The shipped build loads with no error, and the giant's back is seen at Under the Span at night |
| ☑ | After a Sporewood trip, Murk sells Sporewood tackle in Mossmere, and Old Rivals stop asking for a purchase | Back in Mossmere, Murk sells Mossmere tackle and the rival's shopping clue works |
| ☐ | Escaped fish never reach the journal during a normal outing, although the notes say they leave a clue | Either escapes are recorded, or the notes are corrected; your choice |
| ☐ | The legend never gets harder after a landing, and the Odds page shows the wrong odds for rematches | After a landing, her next fight is harder, and the Odds page agrees with the game |
| ☑ | The home stone and eave print count legends sold, not landed | They appear after a landing, with no sale needed |
| ☑ | A legend cannot be kept when the creel is full | She never needs a creel slot |
| ☑ | Her bite hours differ between the game and the Odds page | Both say after the midnight bell |
| ☑ | The Lampsipper's "rises to your lantern" does nothing | The line is removed, or the lantern really draws it |
| ☐ | A report of "an error has occurred" has no steps to reproduce | It is reproduced and fixed, or not seen in five full sessions |
