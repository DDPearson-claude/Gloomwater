# Sporewood at 100%: a checklist for the designer to edit

This is a proposal, in the shape of `mossmere-100.md`: add, strike or reword anything. Each item has a plain test for
"done". A tick means a scenario in `tools/scenarios.mjs` or the timed play-through by script proves it (named in
brackets); an open box needs testers, the device or the designer. "Testers" means three to five people who are told
nothing beforehand, playing a timed session.

## The hunt

| ☐   | Item                                 | Done when                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ☑  | Every beat fires, in order           | From a first arrival the script reaches every beat, from the Drift Float to the ending tally, with no game error (play-through by script)                                             |
| ☑  | The tale and Burr come on time       | The tale at the tally of Sporewood's own evening 2, Burr's lamp from its evening 2, his almanac once, and Murk's two nudges if he is missed ("the tale", "Burr", "Burr missed")       |
| ☑  | Her water moves and is found         | The drift picks a new eddy each evening; moss rafts drift in to it; the island rises within 10 m in her time, once ("spore drift", "moss rafts", "the island rises")                  |
| ☑  | The line-up and the marks work       | Murk names the five crushers; each suspect's card shows its mark and its first catch crosses it out; a cracked pod shows her teeth ("line-up and marks", "cracked pods")              |
| ☑  | Her rules are the plan's             | She noses any bait but a Capseed, a still float and a lure; she takes a Capseed under the Drift Float at 1 in 3, three tries a night ("her refusal and her take", "the thief's bait") |
| ☑  | Each loss names and teaches its part | The Mere Hook crushed, the Plain Knot bitten through, a weak line snapped, each drawn in sketch 5 ("her losses")                                                                      |
| ☑  | Landing pays off                     | Measuring card, release, Burr's Seed, 3,000 into Sporewood's purse and the Glasswater line, on a fresh save and on an old one ("the ending", "old saves")                             |
| ☑  | Nothing gives her away               | No screen at home, no shelf and no field guide page names her or her kind before she is landed ("she stays hidden")                                                                   |
| ☐   | The hunt is the right length         | A keen player lands her between evening 7 and 12 (the script's keen player did not: see decisions.md, "Sporewood's timed play-through"); testers' median in that range                |
| ☐   | She can be found without help        | At least half the testers land her, and can say why they fished the drift's end after the sigh, on a Capseed under the Drift Float                                                    |
| ☐   | Every evening adds something         | From evening 2 until the landing, no tester has an evening without a new case line                                                                                                    |
| ☐   | The seedfall reads as her time       | On a phone, testers notice the Mother Cap's sigh and know it is time to fish for her                                                                                                  |
| ☐   | The two scenes feel right            | You approve the island rising and the crunch, seen in play at each quality                                                                                                            |

| ☐   | Item                                | Done when                                                                                                                           |
| --- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
