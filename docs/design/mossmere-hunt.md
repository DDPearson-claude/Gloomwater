# Mossmere hunt: the Bellhead Catfish

> **Read this first.** The clue design in this document (the Case page, the suspects' cards, Hob's lines, the bell at night and how the Bell Pool is revealed) is replaced by `mossmere-hunt-2.md` and the notepad (`mossmere-notepad.md`). She no longer feeds any time after dark: the midnight bell tells the player when. Murk never advances the Waxed Silk: the player saves for it. What stands from here: her needs, her odds and tries, the landing rules, measure and release, the bounty and the gate to Sporewood. `decisions.md` has the rulings, and it wins over this file.


**The proposal.** Mossmere's legend becomes a wels catfish, the **Bellhead Catfish**, and landing her opens Sporewood. From the second evening the drowned bell tolls after dark and a Case page opens in the journal. Ordinary fishing fills it in: catching suspects rules them out, the Mossbell Isle angler tells one thing a night, and the Bell Pool shows which part of your rig is wrong. She takes only after dark, in the Bell Pool, on a live bait on the bottom, and she breaks any line lighter than Waxed Silk. It needs a new journal page, a voice for the angler, the bell reworked for night, and the gear proposal's Sounding Lead and Live Minnow.

## How it plays

- **Evening 1.** Today's tutorial. At the tally Murk gives you a Sounding Lead.
- **Evening 2.** After dark a deep bell tolls somewhere and a ring spreads out of the mist. A Case page opens. Murk: *"You heard her, then. Hob on Mossbell Isle lost a rod to whatever rings that bell."*
- **Evening 3.** Hob calls out: *"After dark, it was."* A Weeping Carp's card is stamped **Ruled out**. You row towards the toll and the ring rises beside the raft. Where is known.
- **Evening 4.** The mist lifts off the Bell Pool. Worms under a cork bring a swirl: *"Something rose under the cork, looked, and went back down. It feeds lower."* On the lead: *"It nosed the Mire Worms and left them. It wants a meal."*
- **Evening 5.** With a lead and a live minnow, the bell tolls, the tip bell tinkles and the rod goes round. *"Something ancient."* The line parts: *"She rasped the Braided Gut through. Only Waxed Silk will hold her."* Murk: *"Take the silk; I'll have it out of what I owe you."*
- **Evenings 6 to 9.** Three tries a night. She takes on most nights, and you land about half of those.
- **The landing.** She is measured and released. Murk pays the bounty and hands over the Sporewood chart.

This is my estimate, not yet measured: first hook-up around evening 5 to 7, landing around evening 7 to 10.

## The design

### Her
Her model is already a wels. In the show's Chernobyl episode the wels was lost first, and the other catches (zander, pike, perch, chub) match Mossmere's own fish. Her model and fight stay. She is "· · ·" until landed, and the epithet goes.

### What she needs, all at once

| Part | Right | Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Where | inside the Bell Pool | she is not there |
| When | after dark | the bell is silent |
| Bait | Live Minnow (1 in 3); Worm Bunch (1 in 6) | she refuses |
| Depth | on the bottom (Sounding Lead) | Cork Float: she refuses |
| Line | Waxed Silk | lighter: lost once, then refused |

She gets **three tries a night** and takes **once a night at most**. After either, the bell falls silent. With three minnows she takes on about 7 nights in 10.

### The Case page
The page holds a rumour, suspects, witnesses and six headings: **Where, When, Bait, Depth, Line, Sign**. Bait, Depth and Line are the rig picture's own part names. Lines arrive by themselves. When two agree, Murk writes the finding in bold. There is nothing to operate.

| Fish | Its catch card points to | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| Weeping Carp (suspect) | carp hide after dark now | When |
| Milkeye Perch (suspect) | a deep hole they won't enter | Where |
| Reedjaw Pike (suspect) | Hob's line was frayed, not cut | Line |
| Bridgechain Sturgeon (optional) | a bottom feeder, mouth too small | Depth |
| Kissmark Trout (witness) | a wide, toothless mouth scar | Bait |
| Dusk Minnow after dark (witness) | something hunts them | Bait |

Fish caught before the case opens count when it opens.

### On the water
- **The toll.** It sounds after dark, about once a minute, with a ring. Row near it and Where is filled.
- **Hob.** Row close and he gives one line a night about something you don't yet know.
- **Refusals.** A wrong part brings a slow swirl. Once a night for each part, one line names it. A refusal never says what is right.
- **A line every evening.** On a dry evening, Murk's tally line points to a fish or a place, never the answer, and goes into the case.

### Landing her
On the right rig you land her about half the time on the first hook. That rises to about two in three with better gear and earlier escapes. She is measured and released. Her print, a gilt sill stone and the frayed line go on show. The reward is the 900-clam bounty, less any silk Murk advanced, plus the Sporewood chart. Rematches pay 120 for a new longest length.

### What else changes
The Bell Pool is no longer sold; the case reveals it, and saves that bought it keep it. Landing her replaces today's Sporewood gate. The four places that give away her secrets today are rewritten together.

## Decisions for you

**H1. Her bait.** (a) Live Minnow, plus the cheap Worm Bunch (today's Heartmoss pack) at half the chance; (b) Live Minnow only; (c) Worm Bunch only. **Recommend (a):** it keeps your 10-pack and weak-match rule, while caught bait stays better.

**H2. What holds her.** (a) Waxed Silk, advanced by Murk after the first loss and repaid from the bounty; (b) Waxed Silk at 600, saved for; (c) Braided Gut. **Recommend (a):** saving 600 takes 12 to 20 early evenings, and (c) loses the lost-fish moment.

**H3. When she feeds.** (a) any time after dark; (b) only at last light, about two minutes; (c) just after a toll. **Recommend (a):** three tries already pace her; (b) punishes slow rowing.

**H4. Where the bait sits.** (a) on the bottom; (b) under a float set deep, which needs a third depth setting; (c) anywhere. **Recommend (a):** true to wels fishing, and easy to see on a phone.

**H5. Tries a night.** (a) three, and one hook-up; (b) unlimited casts, one hook-up; (c) no limit. **Recommend (a):** it keeps 1 in 3 exciting.

**H6. Landing odds when right.** (a) best three in four; (b) best about five in six, with a harder fight; (c) today's coin flip. Should a loss to a wrong part make her easier next time? **Recommend (a), and no:** preparation shows, and a wrong rig earns nothing.

**H7. The way to Sporewood.** (a) land her, chart included; (b) land her and buy the 2,800 chart; (c) land her and all 12 roster fish. **Recommend (a).**

**H8. Her reward.** (a) measure and release, 900 bounty, keepsake; (b) sell her as today. **Recommend (a).**

**H9. The Bell Pool.** (a) found free through the case; (b) a 1,000 chart, offered once the case points there. **Recommend (a).**

**H10. The case file** (the orchestrator's open ideas). (a) adopt them: a self-filling page, catching as ruling out, a line every evening, clues only from the lake's own voices, opened by the bell on evening 2; (b) the same, opened by Murk after evening 1; (c) no page. **Recommend (a)**, saving the "innocent fish" twist for later.

**H11. Stuck help.** (a) nudges that point to a source; (b) the answer after a few dry evenings. **Recommend (a).**

**H12. The Mossbell Isle angler.** (a) named Hob, one line a night; (b) silent, with Murk retelling. **Recommend (a).**

**H13. Her name and size.** Bellhead Catfish, Tolling Catfish or Harbour Wels? Trim her top weight from 180 kg to about 150 kg? **Recommend Bellhead Catfish, trimmed.**

**H14. Your unfinished camera note** ("The camera angle when unlocking the …"). Was it the mist lifting off a place? The Bell Pool reveal reuses that camera.

### A3. Landing odds (derived)

`p = cap / (1 + e^(−0.1 × (Hold − fight)))`, with Hold = 10 + rod + line + reel + strike (clean 8, struck 2) + 6 × wounds. Lure and bait items count 0 for legends (legend §3.3). [A] The "steady" style emphasis on rod, line and reel is 1; the maps do not give it.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Willow, Wooden; struck or hard take | 17 | 26% | 39% | 34% |
| Willow, Wooden; clean | 23 | 33% | 50% | 47% |
| Ashwood, Brass; clean | 27 | 38% | 56% | 55% |
| Willow, Wooden; clean, 1 wound | 29 | 39% | 59% | 59% |
| Whalebone, Clockwork; clean | 33 | 42% | 63% | 65% |
| Whalebone, Clockwork; clean, 1 wound | 39 | 45% | 68% | 73% |
| Full kit, 3 wounds | 51 | 49% | 73% | 81% |

The cap must be **per legend**. Today `cap.legend` is one value (`Wi = {`, `cap: { ..., legend: 0.5 }`; legend §1.5). Add `Wi.legend.cap = { belled_matriarch: 0.75 }`, falling back to `cap.legend`, so the Drifting Grove keeps 0.5 (Ruled IN 3).

Per evening once everything is right: 0.70 take × about 0.5 to 0.6 land, so about 0.35 to 0.42. The mean is about 2.5 nights, and 90% of players land her within about 5 nights.

### A13. How to know the length is right (hunt-player §9.4, adjusted)

- **Record per save:**
  - the evening each heading becomes Known;
  - the Bell Pool reveal;
  - each refusal by part;
  - the first hook-up;
  - each loss and its reason;
  - the landing;
  - each nudge level used;
  - clams earned per evening.

- **Playtest** with 3 to 5 people who are told nothing about the case.
  - **Pass:**
    - median landing at evening 7 to 11;
    - first hook-up at evening 5 to 8;
    - no evening without a new case line before the landing;
    - at least half can say why they fished the Bell Pool after dark on the bottom with a minnow.
  - **Fail:**
    - anyone gives up on a heading after three nudges;
    - median below 6 or above 14.
